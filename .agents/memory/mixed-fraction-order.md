---
name: Mixed fraction conversion order
description: Whole mixed quantities must be consumed before shorter term replacements.
---

Convert complete mixed numbers before applying fractional term mappings.

**Why:** In Bekhorot 50a:2, a shorter “eight and a half” replacement consumed the tail of “twenty-eight and a half,” producing “20-8½.” A larger cardinal lookup cannot repair that loss reliably.

**How to apply:** Prefer algorithmic mixed-number conversion over enumerating whole-number/fraction combinations. Keep separate rules for unit-interrupted expressions such as “a cubit and a half.” Verify actual source HTML through the complete pipeline, not just plain strings.
