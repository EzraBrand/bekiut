description: User-requested Hebrew navigation and paragraph presentation defaults.

Treat Ben-Yehuda as a primarily Hebrew dictionary: letter grids and previous/next navigation should run RTL. Keep desktop entry width bounded, following the Talmud reader. Offer “Split paragraphs at dashes” enabled by default, covering both em dashes (—) and en dashes (–).

**Why:** The user explicitly requested these conventions after seeing overly wide definitions and LTR navigation in the preview.

**How to apply:** Preserve these conventions when changing or sharing dictionary controls; do not impose English-first navigation on this reader.
