**How to apply:** Deliver recoverable non-public Markdown and distinguish the model's interpretations from the author's views.

Academic bibliographies must discuss the specific passage, not merely the general Talmudic topic. Identify the inspected pages or sections and be explicit about excerpt-only access.

**Why:** The user expressly required works that are definitely discussing that specific passage, rather than general works on Talmud, aggada, or the topic.

**How to apply:** Keep primary comparators and dictionaries separate; omit unverified candidates and report genuine research gaps rather than padding a bibliography.
