# Bekiut — 30-second demo · Shot script

**Ratio:** 16:9, 1920×1080. **Runtime:** 30.0 s exactly (SCENE_DURATIONS sum 30000 ms).

## Treatment
- **Promise:** Bekiut puts the classical Jewish library — and the tools to study it — on one page, Hebrew beside English.
- **Identity:** Bekiut navy #1b4a6e, parchment #f7f1e8, ink #2b2622, corpus bar colors (Bavli teal #4b7a8a, Tanakh green #5a7a4a, Mishnah umber #7a5a4a, Yerushalmi violet #6a5a8a, Mishneh Torah ochre #8a6a3a). Georgia (brand heading face) for display; Frank Ruhl Libre for Hebrew; DM Sans for captions/labels.
- **Hero:** Authentic captures of the live site (white theme), framed as floating "pages" under a virtual camera.
- **Motion:** (1) virtual camera push/pan over real screenshots, (2) masked Georgia type reveals, (3) the five corpus color bars as a persistent graphic thread that becomes transitions.
- **Arc:** quiet letter hook → fast library tour → measured tool punch-ins → map widen → navy lockup.
- **Payoff:** ending inverts to a full navy field with the bet mark at large scale and bekiut.com.

## Shots

**Shot 01 / 0.00–4.50s — Hook.** Parchment field with faint ruled lines. 0.15s a hairline navy rule draws across center. 0.45s Hebrew "בקיאות" rises above rule (Frank Ruhl, 16vmin); 0.9s English "Bekiut" drops below (Georgia, 12vmin). 1.6s logo tile snaps in at left of wordmark (stiff spring). 2.3s caption "The classical Jewish library, side by side." 3.6–4.5s rule splits into five colored bars that sweep left (handoff). Caption bar active throughout film (bottom, 2.8vmin DM Sans) — mirrors VO.

**Shot 02 / 4.50–11.00s — Read in two languages.** Left third: five bars stacked, each resolving its label at 0.4s steps: Babylonian Talmud תלמוד בבלי / Tanakh תנ״ך / Mishnah משנה / Jerusalem Talmud תלמוד ירושלמי / Mishneh Torah משנה תורה. Right: real Berakhot 2a capture, rotated -6°→0°, camera pushes 1.0→1.35 onto the bilingual text block (Hebrew right, English left), 3.2s pan; 4.0s Genesis 1 capture rises behind. Headline "Hebrew & English, together" top. 5.6–6.5s push into page.

**Shot 03 / 11.00–16.50s — Dictionaries.** Jastrow capture front-left, BDB back-right. Big word "Jastrow" at 0.3s, replaced by "Brown–Driver–Briggs" at 2.4s as planes swap depth. Subline "Talmudic Aramaic & Biblical Hebrew lexicons, with abbreviations explained." 4.6s planes fan apart.

**Shot 04 / 16.50–22.50s — Cross-references.** Three real captures in a row: Biblical Index, Mishnah Map, Sugya Viewer. Camera overview 0–0.8s, then punch to each (1.2, 2.8, 4.2s) with screen-space tag: "Verse → Talmud", "Mishnah → Gemara", "Any custom range". 5.4s pull out.

**Shot 05 / 22.50–26.50s — Places.** Places capture full bleed, scale 1.25→1.05; three pins pulse over map area; label "Places of the Talmud and Bible, with sourced notes." 3.5s navy circle iris grows from center.

**Shot 06 / 26.50–30.00s — Lockup.** Navy field. Bet mark scales 0.6→1, wordmark "Bekiut" Georgia parchment, "bekiut.com" underneath at 1.2s. 3.2–3.5s parchment fade for loop.
