## Models and reproducibility

The generation and critical review are separate API invocations through the existing Replit OpenRouter integration. The catalog listed `anthropic/claude-opus-5.5`, described there as a flagship reasoning model; a successful completion verified access. This is the external API’s reported model identity, not a claim about the model underlying Replit Agent or a controlled ranking against all alternatives.

The pilot uses provider-default reasoning settings and temperature, with a generation output budget of 8,192 tokens. The first review attempt exhausted that budget and was rejected rather than treated as complete; the review was rerun on the edited delivery drafts with a 16,384-token budget. An initial access probe requesting high effort returned no ordinary content; a default-setting probe returned content. Therefore these drafts are **not described as a verified maximum-effort run**. The actual returned model, provider, finish reason, usage, and request identifiers are in `generate-provenance.json` and `review-provenance.json`. The review uses the same selected model in a fresh call, not a second provider; this limits reviewer independence.

`generation-prompt.md` and `review-prompt.md` are reusable prompts. `run-pilot.mjs` loads the archived posts and downloaded source JSON, requests generation or review, and stores only ordinary output plus provenance. It deliberately does not store returned hidden reasoning. The `/tmp/llmdan-<reference>.json` inputs can be recreated from `https://www.sefaria.org/api/v3/texts/<reference>?version=hebrew&version=english`. Re-fetching may return changed editions, so verify version metadata on reruns.

`generated-drafts.md` preserves model output; final reader-facing draft files incorporate editorial corrections documented below. `critical-review.md` is the separate model review, not a human scholarly endorsement. The author still needs to review all interpretation and bibliography before publication.

## Disposition of the separate review

The completed review of the delivery drafts identified several useful cautions. The final files adopt these: the opening Bava Batra comparison is explicitly a reading rather than an asserted redactional intention; “foreign convention” is replaced with the source’s attribution to “this nation”; the annual-magistracy analogy is limited; Rabbi Yirmeya’s reply may report a particular case; *heina* and the count of nazirite terms are clarified; the four-wall inference is limited to the listed configurations. The final paragraph no longer projects an unreported lifetime of conduct.

Some review objections reflect the review packet’s limits rather than unchecked research: LSJ, Tobit, and the Gardner excerpts were separately opened and checked by the main research process, but their full pages were not included in the model’s primary-text packet. Their verification links and access limits are recorded above. The Gardner imprint discrepancy remains unresolved. No further model review was commissioned after these limited, source-checkable corrections.
