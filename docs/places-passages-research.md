## Passage matching

The reader uses the same live Talmud search endpoint and section-link convention as the Terms page. Source spellings, including Bursif rather than the modern scholarly label Borsippa, drive search. Alternate recorded source spellings can be selected. Results are text matches, not manually adjudicated identifications; no exhaustive frequency claim is shown.

Verified live responses for Jerusalem, Temple Mount, Bursif, and Beit Ḥoron. Result links include the section anchor when the source reference supplies one.

## Region membership

Regions are editorial browsing groups rather than rectangles clipped from the map. Judea groups the Jerusalem/Benjamin hill-country sites with Judean sites; Galilee includes Upper and Lower Galilee. The broader Land of Israel group includes the coastal plain and Golan entries. Shushan and Adiabene remain outside the Babylonia filter.
